ABOUT ME

Analytics makes more sense to me when there is a real question behind it.

My background combines business analytics and psychology, which has shaped the kinds of questions I am most interested in: what does the data show, what may explain it, and how can the result be useful?

At Dun & Bradstreet, I worked with workforce data, Power BI, Workday, Excel and Python in people analytics. At Elon's Center for Applied Analytics & AI, I worked on applied analytics projects including wildfire prediction and communicating modeling results to a broader audience.

My academic work has let me explore the same combination from different directions, including machine learning and SHAP explainability, occupational fraud research, animal shelter outcomes, experimental psychology and product design. I enjoy the technical work most when it involves investigating an imperfect dataset, testing an approach and translating the result into something another person can use.
