TABLEAU · THREE-PART DATA STORY

Weather and traffic congestion in the Northeast.

For this project, I explored how traffic congestion in Pennsylvania compared with other Northeastern states, and how reported weather conditions and visibility related to delays. I used maps, comparisons and a Tableau Story to move from an overview to more specific questions.

PART 01

Pennsylvania in context

Start with Pennsylvania's congestion and weather events, then compare the state with its neighbors. Maps and trends make the regional differences easier to see.

PART 02

Weather and delays

Look at average traffic delays under different reported weather conditions, including visibility, and compare Pennsylvania with other states in the Northeast.

PART 03

Rain and mist

Take a closer look at delays and affected travel distances during rain and mist using maps and scatterplots, then consider what questions the patterns raise.

Why I organized it as a story

There were a lot of different views in the workbook, and I wanted someone to be able to follow the analysis without clicking through every individual worksheet. The final Tableau file includes 23 worksheets, three dashboards and three story points.

One limitation I would be careful about now: these are descriptive relationships. A longer delay during rain doesn't prove the weather caused it, and comparisons between states can also reflect traffic volume, road networks or differences in reporting.

About the original Tableau file

This page is a written overview of the coursework story, not a live Tableau embed. The packaged original includes a large source spreadsheet and exceeds 300 MB, which is too large for a regular GitHub Pages download. I'm keeping the project accessible here without claiming the complete interactive file is hosted.
